# **Release Notes**

We've been busy building improvements to make your work faster and more powerful\! Here's what's new:

## **New Features**

### **Project Automations – Completely Rebuilt\!**

Remember when setting up automations felt like solving a puzzle? Those days are over. We've completely rebuilt our automation system from the ground up to be ridiculously simple and intuitive.

**What changed?** Everything. Setting up automated notifications and status updates for your projects now takes minutes instead of hours – and you don't need our support team to hold your hand through it.

Here's what you can do now:

* **Get started in minutes** with pre-configured automation templates for each project stage – just pick, customize, and go.
* **Multi-workspace support** – Manage separate automation flows for each of your workspaces without the headache.
* **Smart fallbacks** – Automatically reach teammates via email even when they're not active in-app.
* **Complete visibility** – Track every automation run and see exactly what's working with our new audit dashboard.
* **Personalize at scale** – Use data from your connected tools to automatically tailor every update.
* **Full control** – Pause, resume, or disable individual actions whenever you need.
* **Webhook triggers** – Connect your own systems to trigger custom automation flows for maximum flexibility.

**But that's not all …**

### **Interactive Dashboard Widget – Everything at a Glance**

Your team can now track project health and jump straight to what needs attention through our new **interactive dashboard widget**. Less digging, more doing.
